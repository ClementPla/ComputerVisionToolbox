1D

Explore signal sampling theory, Nyquist theorem, and the effects of sampling rate on signal reconstruction. Learn about evaluation metrics for classification models including accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and confusion matrices.

2D

Understand image quality metrics like PSNR, SNR, and MSE, and visualize the effects of different noise types on images. Visualize the frequency spectrum of images and understand how spatial patterns translate to frequency components. Apply convolution kernels for image smoothing, sharpening, edge detection, and other spatial domain operations. Perform filtering operations in the frequency domain using low-pass, high-pass, and band-pass filters. Explore different color representations including RGB, HSV, LAB, and understand conversions between color spaces. Learn morphological operations like erosion, dilation, opening, and closing for binary and grayscale image processing. Analyze pixel intensity distributions and apply histogram equalization for contrast enhancement. Probability theory

Visualize the Gaussian (normal) distribution and explore its properties including mean and variance.

Machine Learning

Learn dimensionality reduction through PCA, visualize principal components, and understand variance explained by each component. Explore linear discriminant analysis for finding optimal projection directions that maximize class separability. Understand linear decision boundaries and how linear models separate classes in feature space. Explore support vector machines, maximum margin classifiers, and the kernel trick for non-linear decision boundaries. Visualize decision tree splits and decision boundaries in three-dimensional feature space. Draw points and compare how k-Nearest Neighbors and Gaussian Naive Bayes carve up the feature space into decision regions. See how bagging many decision trees into an ensemble smooths the decision boundary and curbs the overfitting of a single tree. Understand loss functions, optimization landscapes, and how models learn by minimizing error. Learn linear and polynomial regression, least squares fitting, and model complexity vs. generalization. Explore probabilistic regression with uncertainty quantification, prior distributions, and posterior predictions. Visualize gradient descent optimization, learning rates, and convergence to local minima. Compare optimization algorithms including SGD, momentum, Adam, and RMSprop on different loss landscapes. Build and train multi-layer perceptrons with backpropagation and understand how deep networks learn representations. Understand how a single convolution operation works, including kernels, stride, padding, and feature extraction. Calculate and visualize receptive fields in convolutional networks and understand how they grow with depth. Compare batch normalization, layer normalization, and instance normalization for stabilizing neural network training. Experiment with a convolutional neural network for image classification on the MNIST dataset. Experiment with a recurrent neural network for sequence modeling and prediction. An overview of the transformer architecture for sequence modeling and attention visualization. Learn unsupervised feature learning through autoencoders, exploring encoding, latent space, and reconstruction. Expectation-maximization clustering algorithm for unsupervised learning, visualizing cluster assignments and centroids. Parametric and non-parametric density estimation techniques for modeling probability distributions from data: KDE and Gaussian mixtures.
