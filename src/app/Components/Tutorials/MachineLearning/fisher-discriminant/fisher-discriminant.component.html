Fisher's Linear Discriminant Discover why maximizing variance (PCA) isn't always best for classification. Fisher's criterion finds the direction that maximizes class separation.

Fisher's criterion maximizes the ratio of between-class variance to within-class variance:

J(w) = (μ₁ - μ₂)² / (σ₁² + σ₂²)

Key insight: PCA finds the direction of maximum total variance, which may align with class overlap. Fisher finds the direction that best separates the classes.

The optimal projection is:

w* = SW⁻¹(μ₁ - μ₂)

where SW is the within-class scatter matrix.
