k-Nearest Neighbors A classifier with no training step — it just remembers the data and asks the neighbours.

To label a new point ✚, k-NN finds the k closest training points and takes a majority vote. There is no model to fit — the data is the model.

ŷ(✚) = majority class among the k nearest points

Drag the ✚ marker around the plane. The lines show exactly which neighbours it is consulting, the dashed circle is the distance to the k-th neighbour, and the marker takes the colour of the winning vote. Move it across the boundary and watch the vote flip.

The role of k: small k follows every local wiggle (low bias, high variance — a jagged boundary); large k averages over a wide neighbourhood (smoother, but it can wash out real structure). Slide k and watch the shaded regions change.
