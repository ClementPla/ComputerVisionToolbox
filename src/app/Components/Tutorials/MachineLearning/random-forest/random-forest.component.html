Random Forest Many weak, overfit trees averaged into one strong, stable classifier.

A single deep decision tree overfits — its boundary is blocky and clings to individual points. A random forest trains many trees, each on a different bootstrap sample (the data resampled with replacement), then has them vote.

Look at the small panels: each tree is jagged and they disagree near the boundary — that disagreement is variance. Averaging their votes cancels it out, so the big panel (the whole forest) is smooth and its colour fades toward the boundary where the trees are split.

Drag ✚: the panel shows how many of the {{ nTrees }} trees vote each way — the forest's confidence. Raise trees to smooth further; raise depth to let each tree overfit harder.
