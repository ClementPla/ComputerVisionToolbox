Normalization Layers

General Formula

y = γ × (x - μ) / √(σ² + ε) + β

γ, β = learnable parameters
μ, σ² = computed statistics
ε = small constant

BatchNorm

Computes μ, σ² over batch and spatial dims per channel.

⚠️ Behavior differs at train/inference time!

LayerNorm

Computes μ, σ² over channels and spatial dims per sample.

Popular in Transformers. Independent of batch size.

InstanceNorm

Computes μ, σ² over spatial dims only, per (sample, channel).

Popular in style transfer. Removes style information.

GroupNorm

Like LayerNorm but divides channels into groups.

Works well with small batch sizes.

When to Use

  • BatchNorm: CNNs, large batches
  • LayerNorm: Transformers, RNNs
  • InstanceNorm: Style transfer
  • GroupNorm: Small batches, detection

Train vs Inference

Only BatchNorm behaves differently:

  • Train: batch statistics
  • Inference: running mean/var

Other norms are identical in both modes.
